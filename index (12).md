# Arcadia Sands

### A New Frontier Awaits.

**A Fallout 4 Frontier Overhaul**

The Commonwealth has changed. The familiar green landscape has given way to a sun-scorched frontier, where survival, exploration, and opportunity await those brave enough to venture into the wasteland.

Arcadia Sands reimagines Fallout 4 through a distinctive desert and frontier-inspired atmosphere, combining visual enhancements, expanded gameplay, improved combat, and additional content.

---

## At a Glance

- **Game:** Fallout 4 (Steam, with all Anniversary Edition DLC and Creation Club content)
- **Installer:** Wabbajack and Mod Organizer 2 (MO2)
- **Profiles:** Arcadia Sands and Arcadia Sands Survival

---

## The Vision

Arcadia Sands is designed to deliver a cohesive frontier experience while preserving the exploration and freedom that make Fallout 4 special.

Inspired by rugged frontier landscapes and the atmosphere of the American Southwest, the project transforms the Commonwealth into a harsher, more visually distinctive world.

Beyond its appearance, the experience focuses on improving gameplay, expanding settlement possibilities, and making exploration more rewarding.

---

## Features

### A Sun-Scorched Commonwealth

Experience a transformed landscape with desert-inspired environments, weather, vegetation, and atmospheric enhancements.

### Enhanced Combat

Face more challenging encounters with improved combat mechanics and smarter enemy behavior.

### Expanded Settlements

Build, develop, and manage settlements with expanded construction possibilities and additional gameplay systems.

### Improved Visuals

Enjoy upgraded lighting, environmental details, and carefully selected graphical enhancements.

### Expanded Content

Discover additional quests, equipment, weapons, and experiences throughout the Commonwealth.

### A Frontier Experience

Explore a world shaped around survival, discovery, and the rugged atmosphere of a post-apocalyptic frontier.

---

## Available Profiles

Arcadia Sands offers two configurations designed for different gameplay experiences.

### Arcadia Sands

The primary Arcadia Sands experience, featuring the core frontier overhaul.

### Arcadia Sands Survival Profile

An alternative configuration focused on a more demanding survival experience.

This profile includes additional survival considerations and configuration differences intended to create a harsher journey through the Commonwealth.
