### Step 1: Start from a clean Fallout 4 install

Uninstall Fallout 4 from your platform (for example, Steam), then delete the leftover folders:

- **Installation folder:** Go to your game's installation directory (for example `Steam\steamapps\common\Fallout 4`) and delete the entire `Fallout 4` folder.
- **Documents folder:** Go to `C:\Users\YourUsername\Documents\My Games\` and delete the `Fallout4` folder. This contains your saves and INI settings.
- **AppData folder (optional but recommended):** For a more thorough clean, delete the `Fallout4` folder inside `C:\Users\YourUsername\AppData\Local\`.

Then reinstall Fallout 4 using your platform's launcher (for example, Steam), and **launch the game once** so it creates a fresh set of INI files and detects your hardware.

> **Set your graphics to High or Medium.** When you first open the launcher, go to Options and set graphics to High or Medium. **Do not use Ultra** or you will get crashes. Fallout 4 is old and does not care how fancy your PC is.
