## Common fixes for all Fallout 4 modlists

### Steam installs the Creation Kit in the wrong place

Steam currently installs Fallout 4 and the Creation Kit incorrectly. To fix this, install both, **Fallout 4 first and the Creation Kit second**. Then open the folder containing the Creation Kit and **move its files into the Fallout 4 folder**.

This currently applies to all Fallout modlists: after installing the Creation Kit, go to its install location and move it into your Fallout 4 folder.

### Missing files

Make sure you have installed all Creation Club content, including any listed in the guide, then launch both Fallout 4 and the Creation Kit one time.

### Disable overlays

Overlays such as Steam, NVIDIA, Xbox Game Bar, and Overwolf are known to cause issues. Disable them.

### Antivirus

Set your antivirus to ignore the modlist folder. Sometimes antivirus software can crash the game.
