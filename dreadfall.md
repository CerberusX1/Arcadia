# Dreadfall

### The Wasteland Has Become a Nightmare.

**A Psychological Horror and Survival Overhaul for Fallout 4**

The Commonwealth is no longer a place of opportunity. It is a place of fear.

Dreadfall transforms Fallout 4 into a dark, unsettling survival horror experience inspired by psychological horror, creature horror, and the oppressive atmosphere of survival horror classics.

Explore a world where danger lurks beyond the next corner, familiar places become unrecognizable, and survival is never guaranteed.

---

## The Vision

Dreadfall is designed to create an oppressive and immersive horror experience.

Rather than relying entirely on jump scares, the project focuses on atmosphere, tension, uncertainty, and the constant feeling that something is watching.

The Commonwealth becomes a hostile environment filled with disturbing encounters, terrifying creatures, and psychological unease.

Every journey outside the safety of shelter should feel like a risk.

---

## Features

### Psychological Horror

Experience an unsettling atmosphere built around tension, uncertainty, environmental storytelling, and psychological horror elements.

### Terrifying Creatures

Encounter dangerous and disturbing creatures that transform familiar areas into places of genuine fear.

### Atmospheric Environments

Explore a darker Commonwealth featuring oppressive weather, atmospheric lighting, environmental changes, and haunting surroundings.

### Survival Gameplay

Resources, preparation, and careful decisions become increasingly important in a world where survival is never guaranteed.

### A Hostile Commonwealth

The wasteland is filled with threats. Exploration becomes dangerous, encounters become unpredictable, and safety is never something to take for granted.

### A Different Kind of Fallout

Dreadfall takes the familiar Fallout 4 experience and transforms it into something darker, more grounded, and far more unsettling.

---

## The Experience

Dreadfall is intended for players who enjoy:

- Psychological and atmospheric horror.
- Creature-focused survival horror.
- Dark and realistic environments.
- Challenging survival gameplay.
- Exploration filled with tension and uncertainty.
