# Community Rules

These rules apply to the Arcadia Discord and the Twitch streams. Not following them will result in removal from the Twitch streams and the Discord.

---

## Community and Jackbox Rules

- No racism.
- No bullying.
- Sexual-themed answers are allowed, but please don't go overboard.
- Roasting players is allowed, but please be respectful. Don't go overboard with it.
- Animal-themed jokes are okay, but don't go overboard with them.
- Some players and members may have health issues, so let's keep things stress-free.
- Please use English only. This is an English-speaking community.

## Fallout Modlist Community Rules

1. When asking for help with our modlists, please be respectful.
2. This modlist is free. I made it in my free time, for my own use, and chose to share it, so being rude because you don't like something will result in a ban.
3. Suggestions are welcome, and a lot of the time they are accepted, so don't hesitate to post one in **#suggestions** on the Discord.
4. I am not an expert on every single mod in the list. If you need info on a specific mod, it's okay to ask in **#general-chat**, since that is not support-related. If you need further help, most mods have a very good user guide on their website.

    Our main mod is Sim Settlements 2, so here is its site: [Sim Settlements 2](https://simsettlements2.com/) (by [kinggath](https://simsettlements2.com/author/kinggath/)).
5. Adding mods to the modlist has the potential to break it, as some mods can cause conflicts. For help in such a case, please go to **#support-rule-5** on the Discord.

## Support Rules

To get proper support, you must do the following. Otherwise I will be unable to provide proper support.

This only applies to crashes during gameplay of one of my modlists.

### What do I do if I crash?

1. **Try to reproduce the crash.** If you can reproduce it, continue to step 2. If you can't, and it keeps crashing a lot at random, skip to step 3.
2. **Write down what you did.** If you can reproduce the crash, make a list of the things you did to cause it so that it can be reproduced.
3. **Find your crash log.** It should open automatically. It is located at:

    `\Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE`

    The file will look like `crash-2026-10-12-200-17-1.log`. The numbers vary; they are the date and the log number.
4. **Join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/3UaP8ssBSv)**, go to the support section, and submit your findings.
5. **Wait for help.** I may be at work or sleeping, so this might take time. Sometimes other members will also offer help.

Want to try some fixes first? Each modlist has its own troubleshooting page, linked from the [Support page]({{ '/support/' | relative_url }}).
