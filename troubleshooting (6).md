# Everwild Troubleshooting

## What if I crash?

1. **Try to reproduce the crash.** If you can reproduce it, continue to step 2. If not, and it keeps crashing a lot at random, skip to step 3.
2. **Write down what you did.** If you can reproduce the crash, make a list of the things you did to cause it so it can be reproduced.
3. **Find your crash log.** It should open automatically. It is located at:

    `\Documents\My Games\Skyrim Special Edition\SKSE`

    The file will look like `crash-2026-10-12-200-17-1.log`. The numbers vary; they are the date and log number.
4. **Join the Discord** and go to the support section and submit your findings: [Arcadia Discord](https://discord.gg/3UaP8ssBSv).
5. **Wait for help.** The list author may be at work or sleeping, so this can take time. Sometimes other members will also offer help.

## How to keep your save stable

- 🔀 Avoid Alt+Tabbing.
- 🚫 Avoid loading saves mid-session.
- 📂 Practice safe saving: disable autosaves and save only during calm moments.
- ⚡ Quit other resource-hungry apps before launching your modlist.
- 🖼️ Keep your graphics driver reasonably current, but test each update. If a new driver causes issues, roll back to the last stable version that worked well for you.
- 🔥 Return any overclocked hardware (usually excluding RAM using XMP or AMD EXPO) to stock speeds.
- 💾 Set your Windows page file to 40,000 min and max, especially for 16 GB of RAM or less. See the [Install page]({{ '/everwild/install/' | relative_url }}) for how.
- 😐 Averaging less than one crash in 4 hours usually isn't a major concern for any heavily modded Skyrim.
