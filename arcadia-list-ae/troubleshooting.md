# The Arcadia List AE Troubleshooting

{% include fallout4-troubleshooting.md %}

## Mod Organizer 2 disabled half of my mods

If Mod Organizer 2 decides to disable half of your mods, just re-enable them. If you need to re-sort them for some reason, hit the **Sort** button and let it do its thing. That is how this list was originally sorted. Make sure **PRP** and **Workshop Framework Script Override** are still at the bottom of the list. They should be.
