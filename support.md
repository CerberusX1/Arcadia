## Common Issues

### Installation Problems

If your modlist isn't installing correctly:

- Verify that you meet the listed system requirements.
- Make sure you are following the correct installation guide.
- Check that required files and downloads are present.
- Avoid skipping installation steps.
- Verify that your game version matches the modlist requirements.

### Game Crashes

If your game crashes unexpectedly:

- Check whether the issue is repeatable.
- Review your crash logs if available.
- Check for recent modlist or configuration changes.
- Avoid removing or updating individual mods without checking compatibility.
- Provide your crash log when requesting assistance.

### Performance Problems

If you're experiencing poor performance:

- Verify that your system meets the recommended requirements.
- Check your graphics settings.
- Make sure your graphics drivers are up to date.
- Check for unusually high memory or VRAM usage.
- Avoid changing multiple performance-related settings at once.

### Missing Textures or Visual Issues

If you encounter missing textures, visual glitches, or unexpected graphical behavior:

- Verify that the required files are installed.
- Check for missing dependencies.
- Review the relevant modlist documentation.
- Report the issue with screenshots when possible.

## Requesting Support

When asking for help in Discord, providing useful information makes troubleshooting much easier.

Whenever possible, include:

- The name of the Arcadia modlist you're using.
- Your active profile.
- Your game version.
- A description of the problem.
- What you were doing when the problem occurred.
- Any relevant crash logs, error messages, or screenshots.
- Recent changes you made before the issue appeared.

Please avoid sharing incomplete information when a full log is available.

---

## Important Information

Arcadia modlists are carefully assembled and maintained configurations.

Modifying individual files, removing required modifications, or installing additional mods can introduce compatibility problems.

Before making changes, check the relevant documentation and seek assistance if you're unsure.

Please remember that troubleshooting can take time. Providing clear information and being patient helps everyone.
