# Credits & Acknowledgments

### Built on Creativity. Made Possible by the Community.

Arcadia would not exist without the incredible work of the gaming and modding communities.

Our projects bring together modifications, tools, resources, and creative contributions from talented individuals and teams who dedicate their time and skills to improving the games we love.

We want to recognize and thank everyone who makes these experiences possible.

---

## Mod Authors and Creators

Every Arcadia modlist relies on the work of independent mod authors, developers, artists, and contributors.

Their creations help bring new environments, characters, quests, gameplay systems, weapons, visuals, and experiences into our favorite games.

We encourage everyone using our modlists to:

- Support the original mod authors whenever possible.
- Endorse and appreciate the work of creators.
- Respect individual mod permissions and usage requirements.
- Follow the original installation instructions where applicable.
- Never redistribute modifications without permission.
- Give proper credit when sharing or discussing their work.

---

## Tools and Resources

Modding involves much more than the modifications themselves.

We also appreciate the developers of the tools and resources that make modlist creation, installation, management, and maintenance possible.

Their work plays an important role in the development and continued improvement of Arcadia projects.

---

## Arcadia Contributors

Arcadia is also made possible by the people who contribute directly to our community and projects.

We appreciate:

- Community members who report bugs and provide feedback.
- Testers who help identify issues and improve stability.
- Moderators who help maintain a welcoming community.
- Contributors who share knowledge and recommendations.
- Everyone who participates in our projects and community activities.

Every contribution helps Arcadia grow.
